# Landing: inside Caesar's workshop

Mode: Experience. Scope: homepage, shared chrome and catalogue materials. User confirmed fullscreen scenes on desktop and mobile, and code-first execution. Preserve content, project catalogue and contact behavior.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A small world of curiosity: the visitor stays on a stage while the portfolio moves around them. Reject the ordinary vertically stacked split hero and portrait card.

OWN-WORLD: Mineral mint #bdd 0b 6, forest ink #19372d, lilac #d 3c 8e 2, apricot #e 6 bca 7 and parchment #e 9e 7de. Bodoni Moda display lettering, Manrope reading text. Clearly woven-canvas colour fields, a tactile C/F/S paper puzzle, and restrained neumorphic controls. The mascot is removed, per the latest direction. Tactile depth is concentrated on controls, with more pronounced moulded profile panels and raised dark experience cards. Project screenshots stay clean; the catalogue adds bounded glass.

STORY: Meet Caesar, explore his interests and real work, understand his experience, then start a conversation. Chapters and direct links make exploration optional rather than compulsory.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Giant Caesar wordmark across the upper stage; three tilted C/F/S sheets occupy the lower centre. Clicking them scatters twelve quarters across the full hero. Manual drag/drop snaps to faint home guides; Putar/Pasang and keyboard offer alternatives, with a stable Susun ulang button. Short introduction and role labels flank or sit above it without a portrait split. A visible project action and chapter dock remain within the first viewport on phone and laptop.

FORM: User-pinned theatrical fullscreen stage, inspired by nesilnoble.com. Desktop scroll consumes active content before a fresh boundary gesture advances a chapter. Compact/coarse-pointer layouts change chapters only with arrows or menu links; native touch scroll is reserved for content. Transitions start immediately with one 680ms animation and deterministic iris/curtain/arch poses in both directions, without a long scroll runway or spring delay. Header jumps remain direct. The ruler and dock explain current progress and navigation. The upgraded puzzle is user-initiated, has no idle motion or Acak control, respects reduced motion, and leaves header/dock accessible. All other chapters retain the accepted behavior. About me has personal Indonesian copy and a pausable type/delete loop. The hero has twenty scattered, locally imported tech logos (fourteen on phones), including all requested brands. Other chapters each have six objects/seven particles (four/four on phones), with birds, nodes, prisms, waypoints, stars and post, all paused when inactive/hidden. Compact music/menu actions reach the right safe edge while preserving44px touch targets. Local music remains opt-in without a lofi hint. Intro auto-completes at 3.9 seconds without skip, once per session; reduced motion skips decoration and makes chapter changes instant. Without JavaScript, all content remains linear. Certificate copy stays fixed while its list scrolls.

FINISH: Review the rendered desktop, laptop, tablet, phone and landscape outputs. Record the actual tokens in DESIGN.md and its sidecar. Preserve user-supplied reference assets and record the provenance of authored vector assets.
