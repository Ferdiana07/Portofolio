# Landing: inside Caesar's workshop

Mode: Experience. Scope: homepage, shared chrome and catalogue materials. User confirmed fullscreen scenes on desktop and mobile, and code-first execution. Preserve content, project catalogue and contact behavior.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A small world of curiosity: the visitor stays on a stage while the portfolio moves around them. Reject the ordinary vertically stacked split hero and portrait card.

OWN-WORLD: Mineral mint #bdd0b6, forest ink #19372d, lilac #d3c8e2, apricot #e6bca7 and parchment #e9e7de. Bodoni Moda display lettering, Manrope reading text. Clearly woven-canvas colour fields, an original folded CFS canvas composition, and restrained neumorphic controls. The mascot is removed, per the latest direction. Tactile depth is concentrated on controls, with more pronounced moulded profile panels and raised dark experience cards. Project screenshots stay clean; the catalogue adds bounded glass.

STORY: Meet Caesar, explore his interests and real work, understand his experience, then start a conversation. Chapters and direct links make exploration optional rather than compulsory.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Giant Caesar wordmark across the upper stage; three printed CFS canvas leaves occupy the lower centre and can be rearranged with a stable button. Short introduction and role labels flank or sit above it without a portrait split. A visible project action and chapter dock remain within the first viewport on phone and laptop.

FORM: User-pinned theatrical fullscreen stage, inspired by nesilnoble.com. No random direction roll: the user supplied and reaffirmed the composition and execution path. Signature motion is a deterministic scroll-linked sequence of iris, side curtain and rounded arch reveals with restrained zoom. The same position has the same pose in both directions. Responsive greeting letters, rearrangeable CFS canvas leaves and a manually controlled project fan supply local interaction. The header owns section navigation; the lower bar shows a 61-tick vertical progress ruler, current scene, previous/next and background-motion pause. Sparse fibres, one shallow relief, and selected contour linework move slowly only on the active scene. Certification copy stays fixed while its list scrolls; local music loads only on explicit play, with a visible off-state invitation. Seven chapters include a dedicated Hi, aku Caesar introduction; each chapter has a reading hold before its gradual transition. Native scroll stays native; no wheel cancellation or scroll library. Reduced motion and missing JavaScript retain readable linear content.

FINISH: Review the rendered desktop, laptop, tablet, phone and landscape outputs. Record the actual tokens in DESIGN.md and its sidecar. Preserve user-supplied reference assets and record the provenance of authored vector assets.
